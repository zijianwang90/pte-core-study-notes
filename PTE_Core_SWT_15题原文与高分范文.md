## 题目-01｜Ontario Community Preschool Funding

### 原题文本

Cutting fund from Ontario community preschool will negatively affect early childhood educator and their families. When funding is reduced, community preschools struggle to maintain operations, and this creates immediate pressure on both childcare access and service quality for local families. Some centres may raise fees or close programs, making it harder for parents to find affordable and stable early learning options.

In addition to limiting access, funding cuts directly weaken the financial stability and career development of early childhood educators. Without consistent provincial support, staff lose wage enhancements and training opportunities, leading to heavier workloads, lower job satisfaction, and increasing turnover across the sector.

Stable and adequate funding is essential to protect educators’ livelihoods and to support the long-term well-being of families and communities. Advocacy groups in Ontario emphasize that investing in preschool programs is not just an expense, but a foundation for social development, workforce participation, and high-quality early education for children.

### 高分单句范文（33词）

> Cuts to Ontario preschool funding threaten affordable childcare access and service quality while weakening educators’ wages, training, job satisfaction and retention; stable investment supports families, educators, communities, workforce participation and children’s early learning.

## 题目-02｜Business Microloans

### 原题文本

Business microloans have emerged as a powerful tool for supporting small enterprises and fostering economic growth, particularly in developing countries. These small, short-term loans are designed to provide entrepreneurs with the necessary capital to start or expand their businesses. Microloans often have lower interest rates compared to traditional loans, making them more accessible to individuals who might not qualify for conventional financing. The impact of microloans can be profound, as they enable entrepreneurs to purchase equipment, hire employees, and increase production, ultimately contributing to the overall economy.

One of the key benefits of business microloans is their ability to empower marginalized groups, including women and minorities, by providing them with the financial resources to achieve economic independence. Many microloan programs specifically target these groups, recognizing that they often face greater barriers to accessing traditional financial services. By supporting these entrepreneurs, microloans can help reduce poverty and promote social inclusion.

However, there are also challenges associated with microloans. Some critics argue that the small loan amounts and short repayment periods can place undue pressure on borrowers, potentially leading to financial strain and default. Additionally, the success of microloan programs often depends on the availability of complementary services, such as financial literacy training and business development support, to ensure that borrowers can effectively manage their loans and grow their businesses.

Despite these challenges, business microloans remain a vital tool for promoting entrepreneurship and economic development. As more financial institutions and non-profit organizations recognize the value of microloans, it is essential to continue refining and expanding these programs to maximize their impact on communities around the world.

### 高分单句范文（33词）

> Business microloans provide accessible capital that helps small enterprises grow and marginalized entrepreneurs gain economic independence, but short repayment periods can strain borrowers, making financial literacy and business-development support essential to program success.

## 题目-03｜Unauthorized Credit-Card Purchases

### 原题文本

**页面提示：** If someone used your card to purchase a car without your permission, who might be your family members, you should contact your bank.

Online shopping has become increasingly popular due to its convenience, allowing consumers to purchase a wide range of products from the comfort of their homes. However, cases of unauthorized transactions, such as when someone uses your credit card to buy a car without your permission, can occur. It is important to report such incidents promptly to your bank to initiate an investigation and potentially recover the lost funds.

In cases where the unauthorized transaction is conducted by a family member, immediate contact with the bank is not advised. Involving the bank could lead to police intervention, as unauthorized use of a credit card is legally considered theft, even among family members. Therefore, it is recommended to resolve the matter privately if possible before escalating it officially. Another situation arises when a friend or acquaintance uses your card with or without your explicit consent. Depending on the circumstances, you may need to evaluate whether to pursue legal action or seek an informal resolution. Each scenario requires careful consideration of the relationship and the potential legal implications.

### 高分单句范文（35词）

> Although online shopping is convenient, unauthorized card transactions should generally be reported promptly, while cases involving family members or acquaintances may call for private or informal resolution after weighing consent, relationships and possible legal consequences.

## 题目-04｜Children and Outdoor Exploration

### 原题文本

Previously, children had plenty of time to play outside, but now many parents often urge them by saying, ‘Hurry up, don’t be late for class.’ As a result, children spend less time in nature and more time rushing between structured activities, losing opportunities to explore freely and enjoy the outdoors.

## 题目-05｜A Successful Business Grand Opening

### 原题文本

Opening a new business is an exciting yet challenging endeavor that requires meticulous planning and strategic execution. One of the crucial aspects of launching a new business is making a memorable impression on your big day, also known as the grand opening. This event not only serves as the official introduction of your business to the public but also sets the tone for your brand's identity and customer experience. To ensure a successful grand opening, it is essential to offer enticing incentives and activities that will attract potential customers and create a buzz around your business.

Consider offering exclusive discounts or special promotions to attendees, which can encourage them to make purchases and spread the word about your business. Additionally, providing free samples or demonstrations of your products or services can give guests a firsthand experience of what your business has to offer. Hosting engaging activities, such as live music, games, or interactive workshops, can also enhance the overall atmosphere and make the event more enjoyable for guests.

Another effective strategy is to offer giveaways or raffles with attractive prizes, which can incentivize attendees to participate and stay longer at your event. Collaborating with local influencers or community leaders can further amplify your reach and attract a larger crowd. Ensuring that your event is well-organized and staffed will contribute to a positive experience for your guests, leaving a lasting impression that encourages them to return. Ultimately, a successful grand opening is about creating a welcoming and memorable experience for your guests, showcasing the unique value of your business, and building a foundation for long-term customer relationships. By offering appealing incentives and engaging activities, you can generate excitement and establish a strong presence in your community.

### 高分单句范文（37词）

> A successful grand opening introduces a business and shapes its brand experience; discounts, samples, demonstrations, entertainment, giveaways and local partnerships attract customers, while careful organization creates a memorable welcome, encourages repeat visits and builds lasting community relationships.

## 题目-07｜Coffeehouse Profitability

### 原题文本

Most successful coffeehouses have heavy foot traffic and high-volume sales. The majority will serve up to 500 customers per day and manage up to five customers turnovers during the lunch hour, despite having limited floor space and modest seating capacity. Profit margins for coffee and espresso drinks are extremely high--after all, you're dealing with a product that's more than 95 percent water. At the same time, your average ticket amount is around $3, so you need volume to reach and maintain profitability. Besides specialty roasted coffee by the cup, most coffeehouses also have espresso-based drinks (cappuccinos, lattes, etc.), assorted teas, bottled water and fruit juices, along with an inviting assortment of baked goods, a selection of desserts and coffee beans by the pound.

### 高分单句范文（36词）

> Successful coffeehouses depend on heavy foot traffic and high sales volume because average purchases are low, while high-margin coffee and espresso drinks and a broad range of beverages, baked goods, desserts and packaged beans support profitability.

## 题目-14｜Esports in Education

### 原题文本

In recent years, Esports has become increasingly recognized by schools as an educational opportunity rather than just entertainment. Many high schools and colleges now offer Esports programs, training spaces, and official teams, showing that educators understand the growing influence of gaming in society. These programs also allow students who are not interested in traditional sports to participate in group activities and feel included at school.

Participating in Esports can help students build confidence, teamwork skills, and a sense of belonging. Team members learn how to communicate under pressure, practice regularly to improve their performance, and support each other just like athletes in physical sports. These experiences are especially valuable for students who might feel shy or disconnected, and success in competitions often motivates them to be more active and focused in their academic life.

Research and school reports show that Esports programs can even improve academic outcomes and increase graduation rates. Students involved in Esports are often more willing to attend classes, maintain passing grades, and plan for their future to stay eligible for the team. This suggests that competitive games are not a distraction, but instead a new pathway to help students stay engaged in school and build long-term goals for their careers.

### 高分单句范文（39词）

> Schools increasingly recognize esports as educational programs that include students outside traditional sports and build confidence, teamwork, communication and belonging; research links participation to better attendance, grades and graduation, suggesting gaming can strengthen academic engagement and long-term career goals.

## 题目-15｜Children Should Spend More Time Outdoors

### 原题文本

Children should spend more time exploring outdoors instead of staying indoors for too much academic learning. In the past, children spent much of their free time playing outside, observing nature, running with friends, and learning from real-life experiences. These outdoor activities helped them develop curiosity, independence, and a stronger connection with the world around them.

However, many children today spend most of their time indoors because of homework, tutoring classes, and pressure to improve academic performance. Instead of climbing trees, playing sports, or exploring parks, they often sit for long hours at desks, using books, computers, or tablets. This change has reduced their chances to move freely and experience the natural environment.

Spending too much time indoors can negatively affect children’s physical and mental development. Lack of outdoor activity may lead to weaker fitness, poor eyesight, stress, and lower creativity. Children also lose important opportunities to build social skills, solve problems independently, and learn how to cooperate with others through play.

Therefore, parents and schools should encourage children to spend more time outdoors. Academic learning is important, but it should not replace outdoor exploration. A healthy childhood needs a balance between study and play, because children learn not only from books, but also from nature, movement, and real-world experiences.

### 高分单句范文（34词）

> Because excessive indoor study can harm children’s physical and mental health and limit creativity, social skills and independence, parents and schools should balance academics with outdoor play, which builds fitness, curiosity and real-world learning.
