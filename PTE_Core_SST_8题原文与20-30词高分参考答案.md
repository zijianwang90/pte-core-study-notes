# PTE Core SST：8 题原文与 20–30 词高分参考答案

> 按 YNWAC 当前 SST 题库逐题整理，共 8 题。题目原文来自页面“显示原文”区域；它是题库提供的文字稿，不保证与录音逐字一致。第 6、8 题页面附有变体/补充说明，文中已标出。每道参考答案均为一个英文句子，并控制在 PTE Core 要求的 20–30 词内。

## 一、作答框架

PTE Core SST 要求听录音后写 **20–30 词**总结；录音约 **45–75 秒**，作答时间 **8 分钟**。官方建议记下主旨和支撑要点，再用简短总结呈现，不要试图复述全部内容。[Pearson PTE Core Listening 官方说明](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core/test-format/listening/)

### 简单步骤

1. **抓主题**：先判断录音在讲什么人、事或建议。
2. **记两到三个支撑点**：关注原因、步骤、建议、结果或对比。
3. **合成一句话**：主题 + 关键细节；删掉例子、重复和个人看法。
4. **检查词数**：控制在 20–30 词；语法完整，意思忠于录音。

### 可套用的句型

> The speaker discusses **[topic]**, emphasizing **[main point]** and noting that **[detail one]** and **[detail two]** help **[result or audience]**.

## 二、题目与参考答案

### 1. Skilled Traders

**页面显示原文**

> Skilled traders are needed around the world. These jobs are good for those who are not good at office work and are paid more than the average pay in Canada.

**核心要点：** 技术工种全球有需求；适合不喜欢/不擅长办公室工作的人；在加拿大薪资高于平均水平。

**高分参考答案（21 词）**

> Skilled trades are in demand worldwide, suit people who prefer practical work to office jobs, and offer above-average pay in Canada.

### 2. Phone Interviews

**页面显示原文**

> Interviewers cannot see your face in a phone interview. Therefore, how you talk is crucial. It is very important to be confident and show your pleasant voice tone and energy. You need to give a short pause and listen to the questions. Keep the conversation going even if you are speaking to a robot.

**核心要点：** 电话面试中表达方式很重要；要自信、有令人愉悦的语气和活力；稍作停顿、认真听题，并保持对话。

**高分参考答案（25 词）**

> In phone interviews, confident speech, a pleasant tone, and energetic delivery matter; candidates should pause to listen and keep conversations engaging, even with automated systems.

### 3. Orientation

**页面显示原文**

> Orientations are trainings for new employees in organizations. Some orientations are boring and a waste of time. Orientations with small sessions will be more effective. Important points: Well-planned training and separating training into few sections.

**核心要点：** 新员工培训有时枯燥低效；充分规划并拆分成较小的环节，能提升培训效果。

**高分参考答案（25 词）**

> Employee orientations can feel wasteful, but they become more effective when carefully planned and divided into smaller sessions that help new staff learn key information.

### 4. The First Day of School for Children

**页面显示原文**

> Remember the names of the teachers and the numbers of the classrooms. Don't forget to prepare money to buy lunch.

**核心要点：** 帮孩子记住老师姓名和教室号码，并准备好买午餐的钱。

**高分参考答案（23 词）**

> Parents should help children prepare for their first school day by remembering teachers’ names and classroom numbers and bringing money to buy lunch.

### 5. Job Interviews

**页面显示原文**

> What a manager is seeking in a job interview is your thought, your motion, and your story, not just answering questions. Additional content: The ability to communicate, describe, and deliver your thoughts, motions, experiences, and stories is important in a job interview.

**核心要点：** 面试官看重的不只是答题，还包括表达想法、经历和故事的能力。页面文字中的 “motion/motions” 用词不自然，参考答案按上下文概括表达能力，不照搬该词。

**高分参考答案（23 词）**

> Job candidates should communicate their thoughts, experiences, and stories clearly, since interviewers value expressive answers and delivery rather than simple responses to questions.

### 6. Cleaning the Park

**页面显示原文及补充**

> The speaker announced teams for park cleaning. Kevin's team will pick up the garbage/trash, Martin by the tree will check plants. Karen has pictures and his team will identify plants that do not belong here. The speaker's team will paint or repaint tables. There are three jobs about cleaning the park: cleaning the trash, checking things that shouldn't be there, and painting or repainting the walls. (Variation: The second task changes to "planets should not be here," and the third task is "painting or repainting all the tables in the park.")

**核心要点：** 页面文字包含任务分工，并附有识别不清的变体；较稳定的内容是捡垃圾、检查/识别植物，以及粉刷公园设施。墙面或桌面的具体说法有出入。

**高分参考答案（25 词）**

> Teams are assigned to maintain the park by collecting rubbish, checking plants and identifying unwanted species, while another group paints or repaints the park tables.

### 7. Clean Foods

**页面显示原文**

> Some foods should be kept clean, and some can only be washed on the outside. Some are pre-washed and do not need to be washed. Use cold water to wash the outside of fruits and vegetables if they have peels. Read the label if they are packaged. Do nothing if they are prewashed or washed.

**核心要点：** 按食品情况处理；带皮果蔬用冷水清洗外表；阅读包装标签；已预洗的食品无需再次清洗。

**高分参考答案（24 词）**

> Food safety depends on the item: rinse the outer skin of produce with cold water, check package labels, and avoid rewashing foods labeled prewashed.

### 8. Rail Pass

**页面显示原文及补充**

> Rail Pass is a train transit card that gives you access to not only the network of trains but also discounts. However, for certain ages, like old people, a reservation is needed, and the fare will be a bit more expensive. Additional content: More access to many railways; do more planning before traveling; first-class tickets will be more expensive. August addition: Listen for "single train," "tickets," "discount," "five or ten dollars." The original text of this article is unknown, and the audio is short.

**核心要点：** 通行卡可用于铁路网络并享受折扣；部分旅客需要预订；某些票价可能更高。页面明确提示原文不详，因此答案只概括这些较稳定的信息。

**高分参考答案（24 词）**

> Rail passes provide access to train networks and discounts, but some travelers need reservations, while certain fares, including first class options, may cost extra.

## 三、复习提醒

- 练习时先听主旨和逻辑关系，再补充两三个关键点；不要把整段逐句抄成摘要。
- 参考答案用于练习信息组织，不是唯一答案；只要关键信息准确、句子通顺且词数达标，也可以有不同表达。
- 第 6、8 题页面注明了转写变体或原文不详，背诵前建议再对照录音确认具体名词。
