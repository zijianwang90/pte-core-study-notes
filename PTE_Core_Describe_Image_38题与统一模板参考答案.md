# PTE Core Describe Image：38 题统一模板与口语参考答案

> 汇总本地原有 YNWAC 的 27 道 DI 题，并核对 [PTE Core Practice 的 36 题题库](https://ptecorepractice.com/practice/describe-image)。跨题库比对后，跳过 25 道完全重复或高度相似题，补入 11 道新题，共 38 道不同题目。每题含题图、图像要点与按统一框架改写的英文参考答案；新站题图保存在同一图片目录，文件编号为 PTE_DI_28–38。

## 一、统一作答框架

这套框架面向 **PTE Core 的高分作答**：先让听者知道图片主题，再概括最明显的规律，最后用少量数据或位置细节支撑。按 Pearson 的考试说明，Describe Image 有 **25 秒准备、40 秒作答**；内容可依次从整体主题、主要信息、细节说到结论。准备时记关键词即可，不要背与图片不相关的整段文字。

### 1. 固定主框架

记住句子顺序，现场只填图片内容。大约七成是固定句型，三成是根据题图选择的主题、趋势和细节：

> The image provides information about [TOPIC].  
> Overall, it is clear that [MAIN FEATURE].  
> Looking at the details, [DETAIL 1].  
> In addition, [DETAIL 2].  
> Meanwhile / By contrast, [DETAIL 3].  
> Overall, the image clearly shows [SUMMARY].

内容顺序：**主题 → 总览 → 细节 1 → 细节 2 → 对比/细节 3 → 总结**。不必每题机械地说满六句：若图片信息简单，可缩短；若是流程图或组合图，可按图型调整中间句。

### 2. 25 秒准备：只写五个提示词

草稿板可用 **T – M – H – L – O**，只写单词、箭头和关键数字，不写完整句子：

| 提示 | 要找什么 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **T — Topic** | 图片主题、对象、时间/地点 | sales, four countries |
| **M — Main feature** | 总体趋势或最明显的规律 | overall ↑, mostly stable |
| **H — Highest / strongest** | 最高值、最大类别、最突出部分 | Canada 80 |
| **L — Lowest / contrast** | 最低值或明显反差 | UK 25 |
| **O — Other detail** | 第二个有用的数据、阶段或位置 | US 65 |

如果图里没有高低数据，就把 H / L 换成最关键的两个步骤、地点或可见细节。优先选能说明主旨的信息，不要试图记下每一个数字。

### 3. 按题型选中间句

#### 柱状图 Bar Chart

先找最高与最低，再选一个居中的代表数据；不要逐条念完所有柱子。

> The bar chart provides information about [TOPIC].  
> Overall, [A] has the highest figure, while [B] has the lowest.  
> Looking at the details, [A] stands at approximately [NUMBER], followed by [C] at around [NUMBER].  
> By contrast, [B] records only about [NUMBER].  
> Overall, the chart shows noticeable differences among these categories.

常用表达：*has the highest / lowest figure; is the second highest; stands at approximately…; is significantly higher than…; the two figures are quite similar; there is a noticeable difference*。

#### 折线图 Line Graph

先判断整体是上升、下降、稳定还是波动，再选起点、终点、峰值或转折点。

> The line graph illustrates changes in [TOPIC] from [START YEAR] to [END YEAR].  
> Overall, [A] shows an upward trend, while [B] [declines / remains relatively stable].  
> At the beginning of the period, [A] stood at approximately [NUMBER].  
> It then [increased steadily / fluctuated] and reached around [NUMBER] by [YEAR].  
> Meanwhile, [B] [KEY CHANGE].  
> Overall, the most noticeable feature is [MAIN CHANGE].

够用的趋势词：上升 *increase / rise / grow*；下降 *decrease / fall / decline*；其他 *remain stable, fluctuate, reach a peak of…, hit a low of…*。优先选自己能说准确的词，不必为了显得高级而冒险。

#### 饼图 Pie Chart

说最大份额、次大份额和最小份额，突出比例关系。

> The pie chart shows the distribution of [TOPIC].  
> Overall, [A] accounts for the largest proportion, whereas [B] represents the smallest share.  
> Looking at the details, [A] makes up approximately [NUMBER] percent of the total.  
> This is followed by [C], at around [NUMBER] percent.  
> In contrast, [B] accounts for only about [NUMBER] percent.  
> Overall, [A] is clearly the dominant category.

比例表达选一种说顺即可：*accounts for 35 percent; makes up 35 percent of the total; represents 35 percent*。

#### 表格 Table

把表格当作分类数据来讲：主题、最突出特征、最高值、另一项有用数据、最低值。

> The table provides information about [TOPIC] across several categories.  
> Overall, it is clear that [MAIN FEATURE].  
> [A] records the highest figure, at approximately [NUMBER].  
> This is followed by [B], at around [NUMBER].  
> In contrast, [C] has the lowest figure, at about [NUMBER].  
> Overall, there are clear differences among the categories.

表格不需要逐行逐列朗读；挑能代表总体规律的数据即可。

#### 流程图 Process / Diagram

先交代起点、终点，再按顺序讲关键阶段；看不清某一步时，用中性过渡句继续。

> The diagram illustrates the process of [TOPIC].  
> Overall, it consists of several stages, beginning with [FIRST STAGE] and ending with [FINAL STAGE].  
> First, [STEP 1].  
> Next, [STEP 2].  
> After that, [STEP 3].  
> Finally, [FINAL STEP].  
> Overall, the diagram shows how [PRODUCT / RESULT] is [MADE / PREPARED / COMPLETED].

顺序连接词：*First → Next → After that → Finally*。某一步不确定时可说 *The material then moves to the next stage* 或 *This is followed by another stage*，不要停下来硬猜。

#### 地图 Map / 平面图 Floor Plan

先说地点或总体分布，再用方位说明主要对象的位置。

> The map shows the distribution / location of [TOPIC] in [AREA].  
> Overall, most of [OBJECTS] are concentrated in [AREA / PART].  
> [A] can mainly be found in the northern / southern / eastern / western part of the area.  
> Meanwhile, [B] is located mainly in [PLACE].  
> There are also [OTHER FEATURES] around [LOCATION].  
> Overall, the map shows a clear geographical distribution / layout of [TOPIC].

方位词：*in the north / south / east / west; in the centre; in the northeastern part; along the coast; near; around; between; on the left / right*。

#### 普通图片 / 场景图

不要套用最高值或趋势；描述主体、可见动作，以及前景和背景。

> The image shows a scene of [TOPIC].  
> The main focus of the image is [PERSON / OBJECT].  
> In the foreground, I can see [DETAIL].  
> In the background, there is / are [DETAIL].  
> On the left / right side, [VISIBLE ACTION OR OBJECT].  
> Overall, the image appears to show [GENERAL SCENE].

对不确定的动作或场景，用 *appears to* / *seems to*，例如 *He appears to be running*。只描述图中可见内容，不推断看不出来的背景故事。

#### 两张图 / 组合图

先说明两张图分别展示什么，再各说一个关键规律；不必把两张图的每个数字都讲完。

> The image contains two charts showing information about [TOPIC].  
> The first chart shows [CONTENT 1], while the second provides information about [CONTENT 2].  
> Overall, the most noticeable feature is [MAIN FEATURE].  
> In the first chart, [KEY DETAIL 1].  
> Meanwhile, the second chart shows that [KEY DETAIL 2].  
> Overall, the two charts provide a clear comparison of [TOPIC].

### 4. 数字模糊时的安全表达

能读清就准确说；看不清时用近似值或比例表达，不要为了报精确数字而卡顿，也不要编造图上没有的信息。

| 图上数值 / 情况 | 可用表达 |
|---|---|
| 37.8% | *approximately 38 percent* |
| 48 | *approximately 50* |
| 52 | *just over 50* |
| 47 | *just under 50* |
| 98 | *nearly 100* |
| 33% | *around one third* |
| 24% | *approximately one quarter* |
| 51% | *about half* |

通用缓冲词：*about, around, approximately, just over, just under, nearly*。

### 5. 作答时的执行要点

- 准备结束后尽快开口；先说主题和总览，保证答案一开始就切题。
- 用两到三个有代表性的细节支撑主旨，避免堆砌数字或逐项报表。
- 说错后直接继续，保持自然语速和音量；不要停下来重来。
- 图片很简单时可以少说一句；信息较多时先保住整体规律和最重要的对比。
- 练习时可用 **25 秒看图 + 30–38 秒作答**，重点练快速开口、近似数字表达和说错后继续。

Pearson 对 Describe Image 的作答时间、组织方式与口语表现建议，见[官方 Speaking & Writing 说明](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core/test-format/speaking-writing/)。

## 三、复习建议

- 图表题优先说清 **主题、单位、总体趋势、最高/最低和一组比较**，不必逐项报数。
- 流程图用顺序词：**First / Next / Then / After that / Finally**。
- 照片题描述可见内容：**foreground / background / on the left / on the right / people are…**，不猜人物身份或原因。
- 先练每题的“总览句 + 两条关键细节”；熟悉后再扩展到完整答案。样例中的数值如写明 about/roughly，代表根据图像刻度估读。
