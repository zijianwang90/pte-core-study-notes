# PTE Core SWT｜38题去重题库、统一框架与高分参考范文

> 本文将本地原有 15 题与网页题库 35 题合并去重，最终收录 38 个不同主题。网页中与旧题相同或高度相似的内容并入已有题目；原有两道户外主题也收拢为一题。范文用于练习，不是官方唯一答案，也不承诺具体分数。

## 一、作答框架

### 1. 核心步骤：Topic → Main idea → Two key points

读完文章先回答“这篇文章主要讲什么”，再找作者的核心观点和最能支撑它的两个信息点。优先保留主要原因、影响、对比、解决方案或结论；通常删去例子、姓名、具体数字、日期和研究过程细节。

### 2. 字数与句式

PTE Core SWT 要求 25–50 词、10 分钟完成，可使用一个或多个完整句子。练习时建议控制在 32–42 词，目标约 35–40 词；信息较多时用两句，句法更稳。保留准确关键词，改写语法结构，不要为了同义替换而扭曲原意。考试限制参见 [Pearson 官方 PTE Core Speaking & Writing 格式说明](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core/test-format/speaking-writing/)。

**普通说明型**

> The passage discusses [TOPIC], explaining that [MAIN IDEA], while highlighting [KEY POINT 1] and [KEY POINT 2].

**两句通用型**

> The passage discusses [TOPIC] and explains that [MAIN IDEA]. It also highlights [POINT 1] and [POINT 2], suggesting that [CONCLUSION].

### 3. 按文章逻辑选择半模板

- **说明型：** The passage discusses [TOPIC], explaining that [MAIN IDEA]. It also highlights [POINT 1] and [POINT 2].
- **因果型：** The passage explains that [CAUSE] contributes to [RESULT], with [POINT 1] and [POINT 2] identified as major factors or consequences.
- **问题—解决型：** The passage discusses [PROBLEM], highlighting [CAUSE/IMPACT] and suggesting [SOLUTION] as an effective way to address it.
- **正反型：** The passage discusses [TOPIC], highlighting benefits such as [ADVANTAGE], while also noting drawbacks including [DISADVANTAGE].
- **研究型：** The passage describes research on [TOPIC], which found that [MAIN FINDING], suggesting [IMPLICATION/CONCLUSION].

### 4. 时间与提交前检查

建议先用约 1–2 分钟找主题和重点，再用约 2 分钟起草，留出时间删减与检查。提交前确认：词数在 25–50 之间；句子完整、标点清楚；主谓一致、单复数、冠词和时态正确；拼写一致；摘要覆盖主旨且没有加入原文未支持的结论。

**记忆口诀：** Topic → Main → Point 1 → Point 2 → 32–42 words → Check。

## 题目-01｜Ontario Community Preschool Funding

### 原题文本

Cutting fund from Ontario community preschool will negatively affect early childhood educator and their families. When funding is reduced, community preschools struggle to maintain operations, and this creates immediate pressure on both childcare access and service quality for local families. Some centres may raise fees or close programs, making it harder for parents to find affordable and stable early learning options.

In addition to limiting access, funding cuts directly weaken the financial stability and career development of early childhood educators. Without consistent provincial support, staff lose wage enhancements and training opportunities, leading to heavier workloads, lower job satisfaction, and increasing turnover across the sector.

Stable and adequate funding is essential to protect educators’ livelihoods and to support the long-term well-being of families and communities. Advocacy groups in Ontario emphasize that investing in preschool programs is not just an expense, but a foundation for social development, workforce participation, and high-quality early education for children.

### 高分参考范文（33词）

> Reduced funding threatens affordable childcare access and service quality in Ontario while undermining educators’ wages, training and retention. Sustained preschool investment supports families, a stable early-childhood workforce, children’s learning and broader community development.

## 题目-02｜Business Microloans

### 原题文本

Business microloans have emerged as a powerful tool for supporting small enterprises and fostering economic growth, particularly in developing countries. These small, short-term loans are designed to provide entrepreneurs with the necessary capital to start or expand their businesses. Microloans often have lower interest rates compared to traditional loans, making them more accessible to individuals who might not qualify for conventional financing. The impact of microloans can be profound, as they enable entrepreneurs to purchase equipment, hire employees, and increase production, ultimately contributing to the overall economy.

One of the key benefits of business microloans is their ability to empower marginalized groups, including women and minorities, by providing them with the financial resources to achieve economic independence. Many microloan programs specifically target these groups, recognizing that they often face greater barriers to accessing traditional financial services. By supporting these entrepreneurs, microloans can help reduce poverty and promote social inclusion.

However, there are also challenges associated with microloans. Some critics argue that the small loan amounts and short repayment periods can place undue pressure on borrowers, potentially leading to financial strain and default. Additionally, the success of microloan programs often depends on the availability of complementary services, such as financial literacy training and business development support, to ensure that borrowers can effectively manage their loans and grow their businesses.

Despite these challenges, business microloans remain a vital tool for promoting entrepreneurship and economic development. As more financial institutions and non-profit organizations recognize the value of microloans, it is essential to continue refining and expanding these programs to maximize their impact on communities around the world.

### 高分参考范文（36词）

> Business microloans give small enterprises and marginalized entrepreneurs accessible capital for growth and economic independence, but short repayment periods can create financial strain. Financial-literacy training and business-development support help borrowers manage debt and improve program outcomes.

## 题目-15｜Compulsory Reburial（网页题 2）

### 原题文本

Human remains are a fundamental part of the archaeological record, offering unique insights into the lives of individuals and populations in the past. Recently, a new set of challenges to the study of human remains has emerged from a rather unexpected direction: the British government revised its interpretation of nineteenth-century burial legislation to drastically curtail the ability of archaeologists to study human remains of any age excavated in England and Wales. This paper examines these extraordinary events and the legal, political, and ethical questions that they raise.

In April 2008 the British government announced that, henceforth, all human remains archaeologically excavated in England and Wales should be reburied after a two-year period of scientific analysis. Not only would internationally important prehistoric remains have to be returned to the ground, removing them from public view but there would no longer be any possibility of long-term scientific investigation as new techniques and methods emerged and developed in the future. Thus, while faunal remains, potsherds, artifacts, and environmental samples could be analysed and re-analyzed in future years, human remains were to be effectively removed from the curation process.

### 高分参考范文（41词）

> The passage examines a British policy requiring excavated human remains to be reburied after two years of study. It highlights the resulting ethical and legal debate, as the rule prevents public access and future research while other archaeological materials remain available.

## 题目-16｜Deep-sea Mineral Resources（网页题 3）

### 原题文本

Although no commercial operations exist to extract these resources, many are planned. ### 高分参考范文（35词）

> Research into deep-sea mining weighs minerals needed for low-carbon technologies against ecological harm. Exploration suggests tellurium may support solar generation, while nodule extraction can damage deep-sea life for decades as demand for ocean resources grows.

## 题目-20｜House Mice（网页题 7）

### 原题文本

According to new research, house mice (Mus musculus) are ideal biomarkers of human settlement as they tend to stow away in crates or on ships that end up going where people go.

Using mice as a proxy for human movement can add to what is already known through archaeological data and answer important questions in areas where there is a lack of artifacts, Searle said.

Where people go, so do mice, often stowing away in carts of hay or on ships. Despite a natural range of just 100 meters (109 yards) and an evolutionary base near Pakistan, the house mouse has managed to colonize every continent, which makes it a useful tool for researchers like Searle.
