## 一、SST 作答框架

PTE Core SST 要求听一段约 **45–75 秒**的录音，在 **8 分钟**内写 **20–30 词**摘要；录音只播放一次，考查 Listening 与 Writing。建议把答案控制在 **24–28 词**，为词数边界留出余量。官方建议记录主旨和支撑点，不要试图逐字抄写录音。[Pearson PTE Core Listening 官方说明](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core/test-format/listening/)

### 1. 听和记：Topic → Main idea → Supporting points

1. **先抓主题**：开头 10–15 秒通常能确定人物、议题或谈话目的。
2. **记主旨和两三个要点**：优先记原因、结果、建议、步骤、对比或问题与解决办法。
3. **听逻辑信号**：`however / but` 常引出转折；`because / therefore` 常连接原因和结果；`first / also` 常提示并列要点；`overall / in conclusion` 常带出结论。例子和数字只有在承载主旨时才保留。
4. **用关键词而非整句做笔记**：可用 `↑ / ↓`（增加/减少）、`→`（导致/因此）、`+ / −`（优点/问题）、`bc`（because）、`imp`（important）、`gov`（government）、`ppl`（people）、`env`（environment）、`dev`（development）、`tech`（technology）、`info`（information）、`edu`（education）、`vs`（对比）、`w/`（with）、`w/o`（without）。

### 2. 选内容结构

| 讲话类型 | 摘要优先结构 |
| --- | --- |
| 解释某主题 | 主题 + 定义/作用 + 关键特点 |
| 原因与结果 | 主题 + 主要原因 + 结果/影响 |
| 问题与解决办法 | 问题 + 影响 + 建议或解决办法 |
| 优点与缺点 | 主要收益 + 主要限制/风险 |
| 观点或建议 | 说话者主张 + 理由 + 期望结果 |

### 3. 半模板

根据 transcript 的实际逻辑挑一种，不要为了套句型而加入录音里没有的观点：

- **主题 + 两个要点**：`The speaker discusses [TOPIC], explaining that [MAIN IDEA], while highlighting [POINT 1] and [POINT 2].`
- **解释 / 因果**：`The speaker explains [TOPIC], emphasizing that [MAIN POINT] and noting that [POINT 1], while [POINT 2].`
- **问题 / 建议**：`The talk focuses on [TOPIC], highlighting [POINT 1] and [POINT 2], while emphasizing the importance of [MAIN IDEA].`
- 可用 `explaining that`、`highlighting`、`while noting that` 把重点压缩为一个完整句子。单句通常更容易控制，但重点是准确覆盖主旨、语法完整和词数达标。

### 4. 提交前检查

- 是否准确写出主题与主旨，并覆盖最重要的支撑点？
- 是否为 **20–30 词**？建议写到 24–28 词左右。
- 主谓、时态、冠词、单复数是否正确？拼写和标点是否清楚？
- 是否删除了不重要的例子、重复细节和 transcript 中无关的赘述？是否避免了录音未提到的推断？

官方评分指南包含 Content、Form、Grammar、Vocabulary 与 Spelling 等维度；内容准确、词数达标，再检查语法、用词和拼写。[Pearson PTE Core Score Guide](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/PTE-Core-Score-Guide-2026.pdf)

### 7. Food Cleaning and Safety（既有题 7；网页题 7；网页变体题 3）

**YNWAC 既有原文**

> Some foods should be kept clean, and some can only be washed on the outside. Some are pre-washed and do not need to be washed. Use cold water to wash the outside of fruits and vegetables if they have peels. Read the label if they are packaged. Do nothing if they are prewashed or washed.

**当前网页 transcript — Washing Food（网页题 7）**

> Food safety largely hinges on proper cleaning, and it's important to know that not all foods are cleaned the same way. Some foods, like leafy greens, berries, and raw meats, should be kept clean. These items often come into contact with dirt, pesticides, or bacteria during cultivation or processing, so a thorough rinse is necessary. On the other hand, some foods, such as root vegetables like potatoes and beets, can only have their outside parts washed with cold water. Their skins act as a natural barrier, and washing the exterior with a brush is enough to remove surface grime. Then there's pre-washed produce. Many supermarkets sell items like bagged salads and pre-cut melons that have already been cleaned. These are labeled as safe to eat without additional washing, so there's no need to rinse them again. For fruits and vegetables with peels, such as bananas, oranges, and cucumbers, use cold water to wash the outside. Cold water helps prevent the loss of nutrients and keeps the produce fresh. If you buy packaged food, always read the label carefully. It will clearly indicate whether the product needs washing. If it's prewashed or marked "ready to eat", doing nothing is the best course of action to avoid introducing new contaminants.

**相关变体 transcript — Washing Food (B)（网页题 3）**

> It is essential to wash fruits and vegetables thoroughly before consumption to remove dirt, pesticides, and potential contaminants. Some types of produce need extra attention when cleaning. For example, spinach and lettuce are often recommended to be washed multiple times due to their intricate leaves. This process helps ensure they are clean and safe to eat. Some grocery stores offer special washers designed for fruits and vegetables, providing added convenience for shoppers concerned about food safety. These devices are made to clean gently but thoroughly. They use techniques such as gentle agitation or water filtration to cleanse produce effectively without damaging delicate textures or nutrients. This extra step can reassure consumers that their fresh produce is not only visually clean but also free from harmful residues. By making washing a routine part of food preparation, consumers can significantly reduce their exposure to potentially harmful substances, promoting better health and peace of mind. Taking these precautions ensures that fruits and vegetables not only taste better but also contribute to overall well-being. In short, spending a little extra time washing fruits and vegetables is a small step that leads to big benefits for everyone's health.

**核心要点：** 食品应按种类清洗：叶菜和莓果需要充分清洁；根茎蔬菜清洗外皮；带皮果蔬用冷水洗外表；预洗食品无需再洗。变体还介绍了商店清洗设备以温和搅动或过滤清洁蔬果。网页 transcript 另将生肉列入需注意清洁的食物。

**高分参考答案（网页题 7；25 词）**

> Food cleaning methods vary by item: rinse leafy greens and berries, scrub root vegetables, wash fruit peels with cold water, and leave prewashed produce untouched.

**高分参考答案（网页题 3 变体；23 词）**

> Thoroughly washing produce removes dirt and pesticides; grocery washers gently use agitation or filtration to clean delicate leaves and reassure health conscious consumers.

**现实食安提示：** 以上保留题库 transcript 供应试复习，不应将其中关于冲洗生肉的表述当作现实操作建议。加拿大卫生部建议烹饪前不要冲洗禽肉，以免细菌随水花扩散。[Health Canada：Poultry safety](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/meat-poultry-fish-seafood-safety/poultry-safety.html)。
