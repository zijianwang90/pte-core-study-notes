# PTE Core RTS：50 题题库与作答框架

> RTS = **Respond to a Situation（情境回应）**。本文根据当前打开的 YNWAC RTS 练习页逐题整理，共 50 题。保留题库中的中英文混排、语法和措辞；题库里标出的“变形/短版”单独列出，便于识别同一情境的不同问法。每题后附英文口语高分参考答案；具体得分仍取决于你是否说清题目细节，以及现场的流利度和发音。

## 一、考场作答框架

PTE Core 的 RTS 会给出日常情境，要求你口头说明在该情境下会怎么说。可记住下面的**结构**，但每次都要把题目中的人物、目的和细节说进去：

1. **称呼 + 说明来意**：Hi [name], I’m calling/talking to you about…
2. **交代情境和关键事实**：The reason is… / I noticed that…
3. **提出明确请求、建议或解决办法**：Would it be possible to…? / Could you please…?
4. **礼貌收尾**：I’d really appreciate your help. / Thank you for understanding.

### 常用功能句

| 情境功能 | 可记忆表达 |
|---|---|
| 道歉 | I’m sorry for… / I sincerely apologize for… |
| 解释原因 | The reason is that… / Unfortunately, … |
| 礼貌请求 | Could you please…? / Would it be possible to…? |
| 提出替代方案 | Would [time/option] work for you? / Alternatively, we could… |
| 提醒或反馈 | I wanted to let you know that… / I’d like to bring this to your attention. |
| 说明不便或问题 | I’m having an issue with… / I’m concerned about… |
| 表示感谢 | I’d really appreciate your help. / Thank you for understanding. |

**练习要点：**先识别对方是谁、你要完成什么沟通目的、题目给了哪些必须提到的细节；回答要切题、具体，并按对象选择正式或非正式语气。别把整段万能稿机械套进每道题。Pearson 说明 RTS 有 20 秒准备、40 秒作答，并按表达是否符合情境、流利度和发音评分：[PTE Core Speaking & Writing 官方说明](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core/test-format/speaking-writing/)。

## 二、50 题题库

### 01. 迟到看医生

**题干**

> You are thirty minutes late for your doctor's appointment. You need to explain to the receptionist and ask if the doctor could see you later today. What would you say to the receptionist?

**变形**

> You had a doctor's appointment at 10 am(10:30) and you were late. Explain at the reception the reason for your delay and request the reception to know if you can see the doctor later today.

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I’m very sorry I’m about thirty minutes late for my appointment. I was delayed on my way here, and I apologize for the inconvenience. Would it be possible for the doctor to see me later today? I’m happy to wait for an available opening. Thank you for checking.

### 02. 租房问题

**题干**

> You rent an apartment and after you move in, you found there were lots of problems, such as broken heater. What would you say to your landlord?

**变形**

> 有三个点要吐槽: 暖气坏了; 房间有怪味; 还有一个忘了

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I wanted to let you know that I’ve found a few problems in the apartment since moving in. The heater is broken, and there is also an unpleasant smell in the room. Could you please arrange an inspection and let me know when the repairs can be done? I’d appreciate your help.

### 03. 超市收费错误

**题干**

> You bought chips at a supermarket with a discount of 2 for 3 dollars, but when you checked out you found a mistake and you were charged for 3 dollars each. What would you say to the cashier?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, I think there may be a pricing mistake. The chips were advertised as two bags for three dollars, but I was charged three dollars for each bag. Could you please check the promotion and correct the total if the offer was applied incorrectly? I can show you the shelf label. Thank you.

### 04. 餐厅点错餐

**题干**

> You are eating in a restaurant, and you order a hamburger, but are served fish and chips. What would you say to the waiter?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Excuse me, I ordered a hamburger, but I’ve been served fish and chips. I’m sorry to trouble you, but could you please check my order and bring the hamburger instead? I understand mistakes happen. If the kitchen needs more time, please let me know. Thank you for your help.

### 05. 图书馆噪音

**题干**

> You have an assignment due next week. Now you are in the library, but someone next to you is playing a video without headphones. You are too distracted to focus your study. You should have a conversation with him to make sure you have a quiet place. What would you say?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, sorry to interrupt. I have an assignment due next week and I’m finding it difficult to concentrate because the video is playing aloud. Would you mind using headphones or turning the sound off while you’re in the library? I’d really appreciate it, and I hope you understand.

### 06. 餐厅遗失包

**题干**

> You and your friends had dinner at a restaurant yesterday. You think you left a bag at the restaurant. You sat at the table next to the window and you have colleague's thing inside the bag. You call the manager of the restaurant and what would you say to the manager?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I had dinner here with my friends yesterday and may have left a bag behind. We were sitting at the table next to the window. The bag contains an item belonging to my colleague, so I’m keen to find it. Could you please check whether your staff found it? I can describe it for you.

### 07. 不想吃意大利餐

**题干**

> You are going to eat outside with your friends and they want to eat Italian food. You don't like Italian food, but want to dine out with them. What would you say to your friends?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> I’d love to go out with you, but Italian food isn’t really my preference. Could we choose another restaurant with a few different options? I’m happy to consider somewhere you like too, or we could pick a place that serves Italian food as well as other dishes. I’d still really like to spend the evening together.

### 08. 生病延期论文

**题干**

> You have an essay due tomorrow, but you have been feeling sick for the past two weeks, you went to the clinic and got a doctor's note. You feel bad about now but have to ask your professor for an extension over the weekend. You go to the professor's office. What do you say to him?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Professor, I’m sorry to ask at short notice. I’ve been unwell for the past two weeks and visited the clinic; I can provide the doctor’s note. My essay is due tomorrow, and I’m worried I can’t finish it to a good standard. Would you consider giving me an extension until after the weekend? Thank you for considering my request.

### 09. 小组作业

**题干**

> You're working on a group project with three other classmates and you have a deadline coming up soon. One of your group members has not contributed anything so far and is not responding to your messages. What would you say to your group member and your other teammates?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, I wanted to check in about our group project. The deadline is coming up, and we haven’t received your part yet. Is something preventing you from contributing? Could we agree on a task you can complete and a time to send it? I’ll update the rest of the team so we can keep the project on schedule.

### 10. 电脑坏了推迟作业

**题干**

> 你是一个学生, 做一个项目, 电脑坏了, there are some visitor to visit me, 需要推迟作业 (新题) 跟老师延期project.

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello Professor, I’m working on the project, but my computer has broken down and I’ve lost time trying to fix it. I also have visitors at home, which has made it harder to work. I’m sorry for the late notice. Could I please have a short extension? I can send you an updated plan and complete it by the new deadline.

**补充说明：**题干为中英文混排，答案按“电脑故障、家中有访客、向老师申请延期”理解。

### 11. 投诉餐厅服务

**题干**

> You have finished your dinner at a restaurant. The dishes are good, but you want to speak to the manager to complain about the waiter service and the coffee you ordered has not arrived. What would you say to the manager?

**变形 1**

> You and your friends went to a local restaurant where you encountered several issues with the quality and service. The long wait times, small meal portions, and the coffee you ordered never arrived. Now, the manager is asking for your feedback after the meal. How would you respond to that?

**变形 2（题库原文）**

> 餐厅吃饭点的咖啡没有来,如何应对; manager asks you how your dishes are...

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Thank you for asking for feedback. The dishes were good, but the service was disappointing: we waited a long time, the portions seemed small, and the coffee I ordered never arrived. Could you please look into the waiter service and make sure the missing coffee is removed from the bill? I hope this helps improve the experience.

### 12. 想与同事一起上班

**题干**

> You are living in the same building with your colleague Mike, and you want to go to work with him every day and share the cost together. What would you say to him?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi Mike, since we live in the same building, I wondered whether you’d be interested in commuting to work together. We could share the driving and split the fuel or parking costs, which might save us both money. Would that work for you? We could try it for a week and see if the schedule suits us.

### 14. 不想吃咖啡简餐

We could choose somewhere relaxed that also serves coffee afterward. I’m happy to hear what you prefer.

### 15. 遗失书

**题干**

> Before we finish, I’d like to remind everyone that several books are missing from our club collection. If you’ve borrowed one, please return it by our next meeting or let me know when you can bring it back. Returning books on time helps every member enjoy them. Thank you for helping us look after the collection.

### 16. 同学家刮大风

**题干**

> Your classmate is on vacation and you are at her home. The strong wind damaged the furniture on the balcony, so you want to inform your classmate so that she could be mentally prepared. What do you say to her?

> Hi, I wanted to let you know about something that happened while you were away. The strong wind damaged some of the furniture on your balcony. I’m sorry to bring you bad news, but I thought you’d rather know before you come home. I can send you a photo and help move anything that looks unsafe.

### 17. 做饭打电话

**题干**

> You are preparing dinner and your friend is calling you to discuss her daughter's problem. You really want to help your friend solve her problem. But you are busy now, so you hope your friend could call you later. What do you say to your friend?

**题库附注**

> 朋友打电话给我讲她儿子还是女儿的事情,我在煮饭叫她稍后再打(9月底更新)

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, I really want to hear what’s happening with your daughter and help if I can. I’m cooking dinner at the moment, so I can’t give you my full attention. Could you call me again in about an hour, or would later this evening suit you? I’ll make sure I’m free to talk then.

### 18. 夜校项目延期

**题干**

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Professor, I’m taking the business night class and my project is due this Friday. I’ve been very busy at work, and I also have visitors staying with me, so I’m behind schedule. I’m sorry I didn’t ask sooner. Would it be possible to submit it after the weekend, perhaps on Monday? I can show you my progress so far.

### 25. 开会迟到十分钟

**题干**

> You are ten minutes late for a meeting because of a traffic jam. How should you explain to your colleagues?

**变形 1**

> You are late by 10 minutes for a meeting due to a broken car and heavy traffic. How do you approach your colleagues or boss to explain your delay? What key points should you mention?

**变短版**

> You are late for a working meeting please explain to your colleagues.

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> I’m sorry I’m ten minutes late for the meeting. I got caught in a traffic jam and should have allowed more time. I know this may have delayed everyone, and I apologize. Could you please give me a quick summary of anything important I missed? I’m ready to join now and will make sure I’m on time next time.

**补充说明：**若考到“汽车故障 + 堵车”的变形，可将原因句换成：My car had a mechanical problem, and the traffic made the delay even longer.

### 49. 食物上错

**题干**

> 和同事要干什么事情，然后你要告诉同事一些信息，这些信息需要包含具体的时间地点…

> **备注：**页面中文标题是“食物上错”，但当前显示的题干与标题似乎不一致；此处按页面显示原样记录，建议之后核对题库。

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

### 50. 邻居包裹寄错

**题干**

> A package addressed to your neighbor has been delivered to your home. The recipient's name is your neighbor's, but the delivery address is yours. What would you say to your neighbor?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, I think a package for you was delivered to my home by mistake. The recipient’s name is yours, but it was left at my address. I’ve kept it safe. Would you like to come by and collect it, or would you prefer me to drop it at your door? Please let me know what’s convenient.

## 三、复习方式

- 先按沟通目的分组练：道歉/解释（01、08、25、33、37、47）、请求（12、18、28、29、43–46）、投诉或反馈（02–06、11、13、20、23、27、49、50）、协商或邀请（07、14、19、21、24、30–32、34、41–42、48）。
- 每题只记 **3 个关键词 + 1 个句型**，用自己的话完整说出来；避免把整份答案写成同一套固定句子。
- 练完检查：有没有回应题目的沟通目的？有没有讲出关键细节？请求是否明确？语气是否适合对方？
