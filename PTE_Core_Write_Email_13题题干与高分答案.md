## 1. Left a bag

**网页进度：** 1/13 · **题库内部题号：** #1

### 题目原文

You and your friends had dinner at a restaurant yesterday. You think you left a bag at the restaurant. Write an email to the restaurant manager. You should write at least 100 words. Your ideas must come from the following three themes:

- Describe your situation, (when, who you were with, where you sat, etc.)
- What the bag look like and what the bag was in?
- Tell them how to contact you.

### 模板答案（原文）

```text
Dear Manager,

I am writing because I believe I left my bag at your restaurant yesterday evening. I had dinner there with three friends at around 7:00 p.m. We were seated at a table near the window on the second floor and left at approximately 9:00 p.m.

The bag is a small black backpack with a silver zipper and a red keychain attached to it. Inside, there is a blue wallet, a pair of sunglasses, and a notebook.

Could you please check whether the bag has been found? If so, you can contact me by phone at 555-123-4567 or by email at example@email.com.

Thank you very much for your help.

Kind regards,
Alex
```

## 2. Ask for vacation

**网页进度：** 2/13 · **题库内部题号：** #2

### 题目原文

You need to take a vacation for 2 weeks and your manager Dani wants you to send her an email with all the details. Write about:

- Exact dates of the vacation
- Reason for the leave
- Who will cover for you during that time and where can they reach you if they have to.

### 模板答案（原文）

```text
Dear Dani,

I’m writing to confirm the details of my upcoming two-week vacation. I would like to take leave from October 20 to November 2 and return to work on November 3.

The reason for my leave is that I will be travelling with my family to visit relatives who live out of town. During my absence, my colleague Michael has agreed to cover my regular duties and respond to any urgent matters.

If necessary, I can still be reached by email at alex@example.com or by phone at 555-234-5678. I will check my messages once a day in case anything important comes up.

Thank you for your understanding.

Kind regards,
Alex
```

## 3. Furniture

```text
Best,
Alex
```

## 4. Furniture 2

**网页进度：** 4/13 · **题库内部题号：** #4

### 题目原文

- Apologize for not being able to assist.

```text
Although I can’t be there in person, I’d be happy to help you pack on Friday evening or lend you some moving boxes and tools.

I hope the move goes smoothly!

Best,
Alex
```

## 5. Susan movie

**网页进度：** 5/13 · **题库内部题号：** #5

### 题目原文

Write an email to Ms. Susan regarding her invitation to go out for a movie, expressing your gratitude for the invitation, and apologizing for your inability to attend. Your response must include the following points:

- Thank her for the invitation.
- Express appreciation for the gesture.
- Apologize for not being able to attend.
- suggest another available time

```text
Dear Ms. Susan,

Thank you very much for inviting me to go out for a movie. I really appreciate your kind gesture and would have enjoyed spending some time together.

If you are available, I would be happy to go another time. Perhaps we could meet next Saturday evening or Sunday afternoon instead. Please let me know which time works better for you.

Thank you again for thinking of me, and I hope we can arrange another time soon.

Kind regards,
Alex
```

## 6. Apartment light

**网页进度：** 6/13 · **题库内部题号：** #6

### 题目原文

1.You live on the second floor of an apartment building with no elevator. There is only one weak light bulb at the entrance of the building and there is no light by the stairs. Please write an email to express these difficulties. You should write at least 100 words. Your ideas must come from the following three themes:

- Install more lights;
- Brighter lights;
- Motion sensing lighting.

### 高分参考答案

**词数（含主题、称呼与落款）：** 115

```text
Subject: Better Lighting in the Stairwell

Dear Building Manager,

I live in apartment 204 on the second floor. At present, the entrance has one weak bulb, and the stairs have no lighting. Without an elevator, residents rely on these stairs daily. At night, poor visibility makes the stairs unsafe, especially with groceries.

Could management install lights on each floor and replace the entrance bulb with a brighter LED? Motion-sensor lights would be ideal because they turn on when someone approaches and save power when the area is empty.

Would you please arrange an inspection and let residents know when the work can be done? Thank you for helping make the building safer.

Kind regards,
Alex
```

- How you are affected;

```text
Please tell me when someone can visit. You can reach me at alex@example.com. Thank you for your help.

Kind regards,
Alex
```

## 9. Company saving energy

**网页进度：** 9/13 · **题库内部题号：** #9

### 题目原文

Your company is trying to save energy, and you want to write an e-mail to make some suggestions to the manager, Mrs. Smith about energy reduction. You should write at least 100 words. Your ideas must come from the following three themes:

- Turing off the lights.
- Shutting down computers.
- Heating and air-conditioning.

### 高分参考答案

**词数（含主题、称呼与落款）：** 119

```text
Subject: Simple Ways to Save Energy

Dear Mrs. Smith,

I’m glad our company wants to save energy. First, staff could turn off lights in empty meeting rooms and offices. Replacing older bulbs with LEDs would also help.

Second, employees should shut down computers and monitors at the end of the day instead of leaving them on overnight. Automatic sleep settings and a short reminder could make this routine easy.

Finally, we could set reasonable temperatures for heating and air-conditioning, close windows while the systems are running, and schedule regular maintenance. These steps should lower our bills while keeping staff comfortable.

I hope these suggestions are useful. I would be happy to help prepare an office reminder.

Kind regards,
Alex
```

## 12. Reservation

```text
Subject: Birthday Party Reservation

Dear Restaurant Manager,

I’m hoping to reserve space at Oscar’s Kitchen for twenty people next Friday, October 2, from 6:30 to 9:00 p.m. The online system only allows bookings for six, so could you arrange several nearby tables or a larger area?

We are celebrating my friend’s birthday. We plan to have dinner, share a cake, and give our friend a small gift. Could you let me know whether we may bring a cake and whether there is an extra fee?

Also, does the building have parking for guests? If spaces need to be reserved, please tell me how to arrange them.

Alex
```

## 13. Alex's invitation

**网页进度：** 13/13 · **题库内部题号：** #15

### 题目原文

It is your son Alex's birthday next month. You are writing an email to invite all classmates/friends in Alex's elementary school to come to the birthday party. You are writing an email to his friends' parents. You should write at least 100 words. Your ideas must come from the following three themes:

- When and where
- Food
- Activities

### 高分参考答案

**词数（含主题、称呼与落款）：** 120

```text
Subject: Invitation to Alex’s Birthday

Dear Parents,

My son Alex’s birthday is next month, and we hope your child can join us. The party will be on Saturday, October 17, from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. at our home, 24 Maple Street. Adults will supervise throughout.

We’ll serve pizza, fruit, snacks, cake, juice, and water. Please tell me if your child has allergies or dietary needs so we can provide suitable food.

The children can enjoy a treasure hunt, team games, music, and time to play together. Please reply by October 10 to let me know whether your child can attend. I can send directions or answer questions at alex@example.com.

We hope your child can join us!

Kind regards,
Alex’s parent
```
