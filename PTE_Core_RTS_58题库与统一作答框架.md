# PTE Core RTS：58 题去重题库与高分参考答案

> RTS = **Respond to a Situation（情境回应）**。本文保留本地原有 YNWAC 题目，与 Chrome 中打开的 [PTE Core Practice RTS 58 题](https://ptecorepractice.com/practice/respond-to-situation)逐题核对；将重复或沟通目标高度相似的题目合并，补入 8 个新情境，共整理为 **58 个不重复情境**。原题中的必要变形和记忆备注仍保留。每题配有按场景、对象和语气调整的英文口语参考答案。

## 一、考场作答框架

RTS 不是复述题干，而是假设你正在和题目中的人说话，直接完成沟通任务。Pearson 当前说明为 **20 秒准备、40 秒作答**；2026 Score Guide 列出每场约 **2–4 题**，评分维度为 **Appropriacy（得体性）、Pronunciation（发音）和 Fluency（流利度）**。其中得体性看你是否清楚完成题目要求的沟通功能，以及正式/非正式语气是否符合对象。[考试格式与作答建议](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-core/test-format/speaking-writing/) · [2026 PTE Core Score Guide](https://www.pearsonpte.com/content/dam/ELL/pte/pearsonpte/pdfs/score-guide-pte-core-2026-01.pdf)

### 1. 五步骨架：Opening → Purpose → Details → Action → Closing

| 步骤 | 要完成什么 | 可用表达 |
|---|---|---|
| **Opening 开场** | 按对象称呼或礼貌打断 | 朋友/同事：*Hi, [name…]*；陌生人/店员：*Excuse me…*；老师/上司：*Hello, Professor… / Hi, [name]…* |
| **Purpose 目的** | 立刻说明来意，不长篇铺垫 | *I’m calling because…; I wanted to ask you about…; I’m contacting you to…; I wanted to let you know…* |
| **Details 细节** | 交代 1–2 个题目给出的关键事实 | *The problem is that…; Unfortunately,…; I noticed that…; As you know,…* |
| **Action 行动** | 明确说出请求、建议、方案或下一步 | *Could you please…?; Would it be possible to…?; I suggest…; Could we… instead?* |
| **Closing 收尾** | 表达感谢、确认或留出回应空间 | *I’d really appreciate your help. Thanks for understanding. Please let me know what works for you.* |

### 2. 通用口语骨架

> Hi / Excuse me, [NAME].  
> I’m [calling / speaking to you] because [PURPOSE].  
> The situation is that [KEY DETAIL 1], and [KEY DETAIL 2].  
> Could you please / Would it be possible to [CLEAR ACTION]?  
> If that isn’t possible, [ALTERNATIVE / NEXT STEP].  
> I’d really appreciate your help. Thanks for understanding.

这只是提醒你“下一句该做什么”，不是要求每题逐句照背。简单题说四五句即可；复杂题可多给一个关键细节或备选方案。人物、原因、时间、请求等信息以题干为准，不要编造。

### 3. 按沟通目的选表达

| 情境功能 | 推荐组织方式 | 常用句型 |
|---|---|---|
| **Request 请求 / 询问信息** | 来意 → 关键条件 → 一个明确请求 → 感谢 | *I’m contacting you because… Could you let me know…? Would it be possible to…?* |
| **Complaint 投诉 / 解决问题** | 说明问题 → 给出具体事实 → 请求核查或补救 → 礼貌收尾 | *I think there may be a problem with… Could you please check…? If there’s been a mistake, could you…?* |
| **Apology / Reschedule 道歉 / 改期** | 道歉 → 简短说明原因 → 提供新时间或替代方案 → 确认对方是否方便 | *I’m sorry, but… Would [time] work instead? If not, I’m happy to…* |
| **Advice / Persuasion 建议 / 劝说** | 表示理解 → 给出建议 → 说明一两个理由 → 邀请对方考虑 | *I understand… You could… because… It might also… What do you think?* |
| **Reminder / Firm request 提醒 / 坚定要求** | 说明责任或影响 → 明确期望的行为 → 给出下一步 | *I need to talk about… As [role], you’re responsible for… Please make sure to…* |
| **Invitation / Refusal 邀请 / 婉拒** | 感谢或表达兴趣 → 说明偏好/限制 → 提出替代方案 | *Thanks for inviting me. I’d love to, but… Could we… instead?* |

### 4. 根据关系调整语气

- **委婉请求**：*Would it be possible to…?*（适合老师、上司、陌生人或正式服务场景）
- **普通请求**：*Could you please…? / Could we…?*（适合同事、邻居和日常沟通）
- **明确建议或要求**：*I’d like you to… / Please make sure to…*（适合需要清楚表达下一步的情境）
- **坚定表达责任**：*I need you to…*（只在题目要求提醒责任、制止问题或投诉时使用，保持冷静，不无端强硬）

口语可自然使用缩写，例如 *I’m, I’d, I’ll, don’t, can’t*。对人说话时用第一人称直接开口；不要说 “The situation says…” 或把题干讲成第三人称摘要。

### 5. 20 秒准备：WHO – WHY – POINTS – ASK

只在草稿上记关键词，不写完整答案：

| 提示 | 快速判断 |
|---|---|
| **WHO** | 我在对谁说？朋友、同事、老师、上司、店员还是邻居？ |
| **WHY** | 我为什么联系对方？这题的主要沟通目的是什么？ |
| **POINTS** | 必须提到哪 1–2 个事实、限制或时间？ |
| **ASK** | 我希望对方做什么？我能提出什么方案或替代选择？ |

**作答检查：**目的是否说清？题干的关键点是否覆盖？请求/方案是否明确？语气是否适合对象？说错时直接继续，保持自然语速和清晰发音，不停下来重录。练习时用 20 秒准备、40 秒内完成；不用为了凑满时间重复同一意思。

## 二、58 题去重题库与高分参考答案

### 01. 迟到看医生

**题干**

> You are thirty minutes late for your doctor's appointment. You need to explain to the receptionist and ask if the doctor could see you later today. What would you say to the receptionist?

**变形**

> You had a doctor's appointment at 10 am(10:30) and you were late. Explain at the reception the reason for your delay and request the reception to know if you can see the doctor later today.

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I’m very sorry I’m about thirty minutes late for my appointment. I was delayed on my way here, and I understand this may affect the schedule. Would it be possible for the doctor to see me later today? I’m happy to wait for an available opening. Could you please check with the doctor? Thank you for your help.

### 02. 租房问题

**题干**

> You rent an apartment and after you move in, you found there were lots of problems, such as broken heater. What would you say to your landlord?

**变形**

> 有三个点要吐槽: 暖气坏了; 房间有怪味; 还有一个忘了

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I’m contacting you because I found a few problems after moving into the apartment. The heater isn’t working, and there’s an unpleasant smell in the room. Could you please arrange an inspection and let me know when the heater can be repaired and the smell checked? I can make the apartment available. I’d appreciate your help.

### 03. 超市收费错误

**题干**

> You bought chips at a supermarket with a discount of 2 for 3 dollars, but when you checked out you found a mistake and you were charged for 3 dollars each. What would you say to the cashier?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, could you help me check a pricing issue? The chips were advertised at two bags for three dollars, but I was charged three dollars for each bag. Could you please check the promotion and correct the total or refund the difference if I was overcharged? I can show you the shelf label. Thanks for looking into it.

### 04. 餐厅点错餐

**题干**

> You are eating in a restaurant, and you order a hamburger, but are served fish and chips. What would you say to the waiter?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Excuse me, I ordered a hamburger, but I’ve been served fish and chips. Could you please check my order and bring the hamburger instead? I understand mistakes can happen, but I’d appreciate getting the meal I ordered. Please let me know if there will be a short wait. Thank you for sorting this out.

### 05. 图书馆噪音

**题干**

> You have an assignment due next week. Now you are in the library, but someone next to you is playing a video without headphones. You are too distracted to focus your study. You should have a conversation with him to make sure you have a quiet place. What would you say?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, sorry to interrupt. I have an assignment due next week, and the video playing aloud is making it hard for me to concentrate. Would you mind using headphones or turning the sound off while you’re in the library? It would really help me finish my work. Thanks for understanding.

### 06. 餐厅遗失包

**题干**

> You and your friends had dinner at a restaurant yesterday. You think you left a bag at the restaurant. You sat at the table next to the window and you have colleague's thing inside the bag. You call the manager of the restaurant and what would you say to the manager?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I’m calling because I may have left a bag at your restaurant yesterday. My friends and I were sitting at the table next to the window, and the bag contains an item belonging to my colleague. Could you please check the lost-property area or ask your staff? I can describe the bag if needed. Thank you for your help.

### 07. 不想吃意大利餐

**题干**

> You are going to eat outside with your friends and they want to eat Italian food. You don't like Italian food, but want to dine out with them. What would you say to your friends?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hey, I’d love to go out with you, but Italian food isn’t really my preference. Could we choose a restaurant with a wider range of dishes instead? I’m happy to consider somewhere you recommend, or a place that has Italian and other options. I still want to spend the evening together. What do you think?

### 08. 生病延期论文

**题干**

> You have an essay due tomorrow, but you have been feeling sick for the past two weeks, you went to the clinic and got a doctor's note. You feel bad about now but have to ask your professor for an extension over the weekend. You go to the professor's office. What do you say to him?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, Professor. I’m sorry to ask at short notice, but I’ve been unwell for the past two weeks and visited the clinic. I can provide the doctor’s note, and my essay is due tomorrow. Would you consider giving me an extension until after the weekend so I can finish it properly? I’d really appreciate your consideration.

### 09. 小组作业

**题干**

> You're working on a group project with three other classmates and you have a deadline coming up soon. One of your group members has not contributed anything so far and is not responding to your messages. What would you say to your group member and your other teammates?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, can we talk about our group project? The deadline is coming up, but we haven’t received your part and haven’t been able to reach you. Is something preventing you from contributing? Could we agree on a task you can complete and a clear deadline? Let’s update the team so we can keep the project on schedule. Thanks.

### 10. 电脑坏了推迟作业

**题干**

> 你是一个学生, 做一个项目, 电脑坏了, there are some visitor to visit me, 需要推迟作业 (新题) 跟老师延期project.

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, Professor. I’m working on my project, but my computer has broken down and I’ve lost time trying to fix it. I also have visitors at home, which has made it harder to work. I’m sorry for the late notice. Could I please have a short extension until [date]? I can share my progress and an updated plan. Thank you for considering it.

**补充说明：**题干为中英文混排，答案按“电脑故障、家中有访客、向老师申请延期”理解。

### 11. 投诉餐厅服务

**题干**

> You have finished your dinner at a restaurant. The dishes are good, but you want to speak to the manager to complain about the waiter service and the coffee you ordered has not arrived. What would you say to the manager?

**变形 1**

> You and your friends went to a local restaurant where you encountered several issues with the quality and service. The long wait times, small meal portions, and the coffee you ordered never arrived. Now, the manager is asking for your feedback after the meal. How would you respond to that?

**变形 2（题库原文）**

> 餐厅吃饭点的咖啡没有来,如何应对; manager asks you how your dishes are...

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Thank you for asking for feedback. The dishes were good, but the service was disappointing: we waited a long time, the portions seemed small, and the coffee I ordered never arrived. Could you please check what happened to the coffee and remove it from the bill? I also wanted to bring the wait and portion sizes to your attention. Thank you for listening.

### 12. 想与同事一起上班

**题干**

> You are living in the same building with your colleague Mike, and you want to go to work with him every day and share the cost together. What would you say to him?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi Mike, since we live in the same building, would you be interested in commuting to work together? We could share the driving and split the fuel or parking costs, which might save us both money. What time do you usually leave, and would you like to try it for a week? Let me know what works for you. Thanks.

### 13. 图书馆借书缺页

**题干**

> You borrowed a book from the university library, but while reading it, you notice that two pages have been torn out, but you didn't do it. What do you say to the librarian when you return the book?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, I’m returning this library book and need to report that two pages were torn out when I was reading it. I didn’t damage the book, so I wanted to let you know before returning it. Could you please make a note of its condition and tell me if you need any other information? Thank you for your help.

### 14. 不想吃咖啡简餐

**题干**

> You have a dinner date with a friend, and she wants to go to a cafe where only serves coffee, tea, etc. You want to eat in a restaurant with a lot of foods. What do you say to your friend? (sam's cafe)

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hey, I’m looking forward to dinner with you, but Sam’s Cafe mainly serves coffee, tea, and light refreshments, and I’m hoping to have a proper meal. Would you be open to trying a nearby restaurant with more food options? We could still have coffee there or afterward. What would you prefer?

### 15. 遗失书

**题干**

> You're leading a community book club, and during this month's meeting, you discover several books are missing from the club's library. You want to address the issue respectfully, reminding members to return books on time for everyone's benefit. What would you say to your members?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Before we finish, I’d like to remind everyone that several books are missing from our club collection. If you’ve borrowed one, please return it by our next meeting or let me know when you can bring it back. Returning books on time means every member can enjoy them. Could everyone check for any club books at home? Thank you.

### 16. 同学家刮大风

**题干**

> Your classmate is on vacation and you are at her home. The strong wind damaged the furniture on the balcony, so you want to inform your classmate so that she could be mentally prepared. What do you say to her?

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, I wanted to let you know what happened while you were away. The strong wind damaged some of the furniture on your balcony. I’m sorry to bring you bad news, but I thought you’d want to know before you come home. I can send you a photo and help move anything that looks unsafe. Please let me know how I can help.

### 17. 做饭打电话

**题干**

> You are preparing dinner and your friend is calling you to discuss her daughter's problem. You really want to help your friend solve her problem. But you are busy now, so you hope your friend could call you later. What do you say to your friend?

**题库附注**

> 朋友打电话给我讲她儿子还是女儿的事情,我在煮饭叫她稍后再打(9月底更新)

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hi, I really want to hear about your daughter’s problem and help if I can. I’m cooking dinner at the moment, so I can’t give you my full attention. Could you call me again in about an hour, or would later this evening suit you better? I’ll make sure I’m free to talk then. Thanks for understanding.

### 18. 夜校项目延期

**题干**

> You are taking business night classes and your project is going to due soon. You want to ask your professor for an extension over the weekend. You go to the professor's office. What do you say to him?

**变形/补充要点**

> 申请延期, project due on Friday, 申请理由: have some visitors, busy at work

**高分参考答案（口语示例）**

> Hello, Professor. My business-class project is due this Friday, and I’m behind because I’ve been busy at work and have visitors staying with me. I’m sorry I didn’t ask sooner. Would it be possible to submit it after the weekend, perhaps on Monday? I can show you the progress I’ve made and finish by the new deadline. Thank you.

### 19. 不能加入体育俱乐部

### 21. 头疼不能看电影

### 25. 开会迟到十分钟

**题干**

> You are ten minutes late for a meeting because of a traffic jam. How should you explain to your colleagues?

**变形 1**

### 31. 朋友约散步怕下雨

**题干**

> Your friend Susan/Jim, suggest having a walk in the countryside tomorrow. You would love to, but you have seen the weather forecast and it might rain tomorrow. You want to wait and see the weather tomorrow morning, and decide then. What would you say to Susan right now?

## 三、复习方式

- 按沟通目的练习，而不是按固定句子背诵：道歉/解释/改期（01、08、10、17、18、21、25、26、31、33、37、41、43、47）；请求/询问信息（02、06、09、12、13、22、28、29、30、34、35、36、38、40、44、45、46、48、49、51、53、54、55、58）；投诉/纠错/提醒（03、04、05、11、15、20、23、27、39、50、56）；建议/协商/说服（07、14、16、19、24、32、42、52、57）。
- 20 秒准备时写 **WHO – WHY – POINTS – ASK**；答案里明确目的、题目细节和要对方采取的行动。
- 每题先练 30–40 秒自然口语，再检查：是否完成任务、语气是否符合关系、关键细节是否清楚、有没有停顿或重复。
- 可以记住表达模块，但要按对象和沟通目的选择；不必每题从头到尾照搬同一段模板。
